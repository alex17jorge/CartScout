League of Legends V26.11

[edit | edit source]

Client

[edit | edit source]
Blue Essence Emporium
  • Bug Fix: Fixed bugs in the Blue Essence Emporium that caused us to disable the purchase of Mystery Icons and Mystery Wards.
Ranked
  • Bug Fix: Fixed several issues where players wouldn't receive consolation LP when they were supposed to for terminated matches, AFKs, verified disruptive behavior, etc.
Ranked Leaderboards
  • Removed the "My Leagues" feature that displays where you are relative to random players in the same tier and division. You can still see how you're doing compared to your friends.
  • You'll still be able to see Master+ ladders.

Game

[edit | edit source]
Clamp Casting
  • Bug Fix: Adjustments have been made to improve clamp casting. Players will no longer walk slightly backwards when casting with clamp casting enabled. Point and Click players will experience faster casting, and WASD players will experience more consistent casting.
Role Quests
  • Midlane Quest
    • Bonus attack damage increased to 8% bonus AD from 6%.
    • Bonus ability power increased to 8% AP from 6% AP.
  • Support Quest
    • Bug Fix: Fixed a bug where supports could not undo after purchasing a Control Ward.
WASD Controls
  • Bug Fix: Further improvements have been made to auto attacking. Controls are more responsive when champions start and stop auto attacking the same target, or when frequently stepping in and out of range of the same target.
  • Bug Fix: Once the control scheme change time limit has passed, players will no longer be able to swap control schemes even if they have not yet changed their control scheme during the game.
  • Bug Fix: Fixed a bug where it was difficult to reset your auto attack with Master Yi's Meditate.
  • Bug Fix: Fixed a bug that caused clamp casting to be applied when it shouldn't be for items like ward trinkets and Redemption. This matches the behavior in Point and Click.

League of Legends
League of Legends (or LoL; initially League of Legends: Clash of Fates) is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) genre video game developed by Riot Games.

Platform
  • Microsoft Windows, macOS
Regular Map
  • Summoner's Rift

League of Legends content
Midlane Quest
Earn 1350 Points:
  • Champion takedowns (25 points)
  • Minion kills (2 points in mid lane, 25% - 100% of that in other lanes)
  • Turret takedowns (50 points in mid lane, 25% - 100% of that in other lanes)
  • Destroy turret plates (40 points from mid lane turrets, 25% - 100% of that in other lanes)
  • Epic monster takedowns (30 points)
  • Gain 1 point every 3 seconds starting at 1:05, increased to 7.5 every 5 in mid lane.
  • Gain ( 3% / 1.5%) of damage dealt to champions as points.

Rewards:

  • Gain 8% bonus AD and 8% AP.
  • Tier 2 Boots are upgraded to exclusive Tier 3 Boots at no cost. This applies proactively to any Tier 2 Boots equipped at any point.
    • Berserker's Greaves is upgraded into Gunmetal Greaves.
    • Boots of Swiftness is upgraded into Swiftmarch.
    • Gluttonous Greaves is upgraded into Immortal Path.
    • Ionian Boots of Lucidity is upgraded into Crimson Lucidity.
    • Mercury's Treads is upgraded into Chainlaced Crushers.
    • Plated Steelcaps is upgraded into Armored Advance.
    • Sorcerer's Shoes is upgraded into Spellslinger's Shoes.

Gold and experience from minions is reduced by 25% outside of mid lane until level 3.

Support Quest

Earn 800 Points:

  • Correlates with the Support Quest of the World Atlas and Runic Compass support items. Gain points equal to the exact amount of gold earned through the support quest.
    • Consume a Shared Riches charge by dealing damage to champions or turrets:
      • World Atlas: Gain ( 22 / 20) points
      • Runic Compass: Gain ( 24 / 22) points
    • Consume a Shared Riches charge by killing minions:
      • World Atlas: Gain 15 points
      • Runic Compass: Gain 20 points
    • Earn gold through the gold generation stat:
      • World Atlas: Gain 3 points per 10 seconds
      • Runic Compass: Gain 5 points per 10 seconds

Rewards:

  • Upgrade Runic Compass into Bounty of Worlds, granting access to the Upgrade effect which allows Bounty of Worlds to be upgraded into Bloodsong, Celestial Opposition, Dream Maker, Solstice Sleigh, or Zaz'Zak's Realmspike for free.
  • Gain a 7th item slot in the Role Quest slot that is reserved for and stores up to 2 Control Wards.
    • This reward is granted at the start of the game, prior to quest completion.
  • Control Ward cost at the shop reduced to 40.

Gold and experience from minion kills and from Support Quest items is reduced by 25% outside of bot lane until level 3.

Immobilize

Immobilized units cannot control their movement, becoming unable to input movement commands and use movement spells. The following crowd control effects immobilize the unit:

  • Airborne
  • Forced Action ( Berserk, Charm, Flee, Taunt)
  • Root
  • Sleep
  • Stasis
  • Stun ( Suspension)
  • Suppression

Impaired movement is a similar term that, in addition to the above, also refers to units that are slowed.

Centered range

Centered range is calculated from the center of a source unit's hitbox to the center of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Centered range is used for most unit-targeted abilities.
  • It is less effective and may seem to have diminished reach against large units.
League of Legends content
Edge range

Edge range is calculated from the edge of a source unit's hitbox to the edge of a target unit's hitbox.

  • Edge range is mostly used for basic attacks; skill shots; and point-blank areas of effect (including auras).
  • These become more effective against large champions, as the source gains more reach against them. This may be favorable or unfavorable: durable champions tend to be larger and thus easier to hit, and frail champions tend to be smaller and more difficult to hit.

Area damage

Area damage is the damage type dealt by most abilities and some items and runes that can hit targets within a zone. Area damage applies spell effects by default.

  • Jax's Counter Strike grants him 25% damage reduction against all incoming area damage.
  • Many champion summoned units such as Elise's Spiderlings, Naafiri's Packmates, and Yorick's Mist Walkers take reduced damage from area damage effects.
  •  Omnivamp heals with 33.3% effectiveness on instances of area damage against non-champions.

Effects triggered by damage subtypes ( physical, magic, true) will always occur regardless of damage type (e.g. Black Cleaver's Carve stacking on instances of physical damage and Morellonomicon's Grievous Wounds application on instances of magic damage).

Ranged

Ranged champions have a basic attack  range usually beyond 300 units. Many deal damage through projectiles, which are subject to mitigating effects like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for ranged characters.

League of Legends content
Basic attack
The basic attack (or "auto") is the default means by which a unit deals damage, usually equaling  100% AD physical damage. Basic attacks are normally unit-targeted edge range spells, and the  attack range stat determines their reach.

The caster must stand still to complete a brief windup before launching a basic attack. Depending on the caster:

  • If a caster's attacks are non-projectile (e.g. melee) launching the attack deals damage immediately.
  • If a caster's attacks are projectile, launching the attack sends a projectile that follows the target and deals damage upon striking them.
The caster then incurs a brief cooldown, based on their  attack speed. Basic attacks' damage may trigger on-hit effects only upon dealing damage. On-attack effects will always trigger upon the attack's windup completing.

League of Legends content
Cast instance

Cast instance is the identifier that tells the source of spells; it is the game's method of associating multiple effects (damage events, buff applications) as originating from a specific, singular attack or ability cast by a unit. Multiple effects occurring under the same cast instance behave as one for certain interactions. Some notable examples:

  • Some of Conqueror's stacking can only be triggered once per cast instance.
  • Electrocute and Eclipse can only receive 1 stack for its activation per cast instance.
  • Muramana deals its damage once per cast instance.

Not to be confused with damage instance, which is each individual effects, even under the same cast instance.

League of Legends content
Slow
A unit that is slowed has reduced  movement speed for the duration. Slows based on a percentage value cannot reduce a target below  110 movement speed. Some slows will instead modify the target's movement speed to a static value, which may not be further reduced nor increased.

Only the slow with the highest value is taken into movement speed calculations. All additional slows are ignored until the higher-valued slow wears off. The strength of slows may amplify or decay over their duration. Slows of the same debuff cannot stack and will instead refresh on each application unless otherwise stated.

Negative slow values will increase the unit's movement speed instead.

Interrupts: Does not interrupt any actions.
Reduction: The duration is affected by Tenacity.

The strength is reduced by slow resist.

Resist: Resisted by crowd control immunity and slow immunity.
Removal: Removed by Cleanses, Garen's Decisive Strike, Master Yi's Highlander and Nunu's Biggest Snowball Ever!.
League of Legends content
Melee

Melee champions have a basic attack  range usually within 200 units. A melee attack does not use a projectile to deal damage, therefore not subject to effects that mitigate projectiles, like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for melee characters.
