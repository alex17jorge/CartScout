League of Legends V26.14

[edit | edit source]

Client

[edit | edit source]
Clash
  • The MSI Cup will be taking place on Summoner's Rift this patch.
    • Registration Begins: July 13, 2026 (11:00AM Local Time).
    • Tournament Dates: July 18 and 19, 2026 (~ 4 - 7 PM Local Time, varies by region).
Parental Controls
  • Riot is rolling out optional Parent Permissions to give parents more visibility and control over their child's social interactions in game. Parents can access parental controls by connecting a parent's email address via account control page, where they can limit text and voice chat, block friend requests, and view the social panel friends list. These controls are meant to provide parents with the tools to protect their child's safety.
Ranked
  • Ranked Season 2 will end local server time at midnight 23:59 on July 28, 2026, which is the last day of patch 26.14. Several hours later, we'll roll over into patch 26.15, which marks the start of Season 3 at 12:00pm noon, July 29, 2026, local server time.
  • Similar to previous mid-year season transitions, Ranked Solo and Flex queues will be disabled during the down time. Shard transfer services will also be disabled to make sure rewards are properly calculated.
  • We don't expect any disruptions to your LP / Ranked progression, but the Victorious Ranked mission for 15 ranked games won will be refreshed when the new season starts.
T1 2025 Worlds Winners
  • In honor of League's first three-peat world champions, the T1 Worlds Winner skins will follow a slightly different release approach. The Signature Bundles will include the skin, signature icon and border, ward skin, and Elite chroma. As a special bonus, each bundle will also include an exclusive emote inspired by the pros and the skin they helped design. The Worlds Winner Megabundle will include all Signature Bundles, and three megabundle exclusive items: Golden Team Icon, T1 2025 Worlds Winner Lobby Banner, and the "Three-peat, 1 Dynasty" Title. All bundles will be available for purchase for two patches, going away at the start of Patch 26.16.
  • All skins will also be individually purchasable with RP. T1 Ambessa, Xin Zhao, Galio, Yunara, and Seraphine will cost 1350 RP. The MVP skin this year, MVP T1 Miss Fortune, will also be individually purchasable for 1820 RP. Individual skins will be available in the Shop until the start of patch 26.23.
  • Once MVP T1 Miss Fortune leaves the Shop, she will be entered into the Mythic Shop pool in the first patch of 2027. She will have a chance to appear in the rotating or featured section, and she will be priced at 200 Mythic Essence. Acquiring MVP T1 Miss Fortune from the Mythic Shop will not include her Signature Icon or Border. The other Epic skins will leave the store and enter the Loot pool starting with the first patch of 2027.
  • In addition to the Elite chromas, there will also be 5 standard chromas available for purchase. The Ruby chroma will be a chroma bundle exclusive, and the others will be available for individual purchase.
  • Last, but certainly not least, swing by the Mythic Shop to snag the "Together as 1" Nexus Finisher for 250 Mythic Essence. It will be in the Featured section for two patches, leaving at the start of Patch 26.16.
  • Riot will share a portion of proceeds with LoL esports teams.

Game

[edit | edit source]
Buffs
  • Trimmed redundant buffs that repeat information already clearly communicated elsewhere in the UI or gameplay state (e.g. role quest progress and other obvious state changes).
  • Moved most item buffs with durations into the inventory on the item itself.
  • Reduced buff bar clutter from lower-impact system, map and item effects that don't meaningfully change player decision-making.
  • Removed display of very short-duration debuffs and effects (˜0.25s), including things like minor slows from Honeyfruit pickups.
Mute
  • Bug Fix: Deafen and self-mute functionality can now only be toggled once every 60 seconds to prevent spam and disruptive one-way communication.
Remake
  • Bug Fix: Remake votes will now begin automatically when they are eligible rather than requiring a player to type /remake in chat. Chat context now explains which player triggered the remake vote and why.

League of Legends content
Airborne

A unit that is knocked airborne undergoes a forced displacement in a specified direction, to a specific point, or for a set duration at its current location, during which it is also unable to control its movement, declare attacks, cast abilities, activate items or use the summoner spells Flash, Teleport, Hexflash, and Mark / Dash.
Airborne is an immobilizing effect.

Interrupts: Interrupts attack windups (excluding uncancellable windups), and all channeled and charged abilities. The accompanying displacement overrides all other previous displacements, including dashes and other airborne effects.
Reduction: The duration of airborne and its forced movement execution time is unaffected by Tenacity.
Resist: Resisted by crowd control immunity, displacement immunity, and Dr. Mundo's Goes Where He Pleases.
Removal: The forced movement that is applied alongside airborne effects cannot be removed by cleanses, which innately disables attacking and moving and can be overridden by dashing, blinking or lunging. Cleansing effects, with the exceptions of Milio's Breath of Life, Cleanse, Mikael's Blessing, and Quicksilver, remove the airborne status effect.

Crowd control applied by one's self or an ally cannot be countered in any way, neither reduced in duration, resisted, nor removed unless special cased to do so.

  • The crowd control effect may still be cleansed if it is applied as a debuff separate from the originated ability.

Recast: Ritual Nails can be recast twice more within a few seconds to mimic the first cast's effects, after which the  cooldown and  cost is partially refunded for each leftover recast.
Locke's next basic attack or Ashen Pursuit against an enemy with Soul Nails stacks consumes them all to deal bonus magic damage, increased for each stack.
League of Legends content
 Soul Ignition COOLDOWN: 18 / 17 / 16 / 15 / 14
Active: Locke ignites his soul for a few seconds, gaining  bonus attack speed and  bonus move speed. He also stores grey health equal to the damage taken from champions, and an additional amount based on his missing health and Soul Ignition's elapsed duration.
Soul Ignition can be recast within the duration, and does so automatically afterwards. The recast can be used while under cast-inhibiting crowd control.
Recast: Locke ends Soul Ignition and consumes his grey health to heal for the same amount.
Soul Ignition incurs a continual health cost over the duration, which is then compensated in the heal.
League of Legends content
 Death's Grasp COOLDOWN: 18 / 16 / 14 / 12 / 10
Passive: Mordekaiser gains  magic penetration.
Active: Mordekaiser summons a claw in the target direction that grants sight of the area. After a brief time, it deals magic damage and pulls enemies hit.
League of Legends content
 Realm of Death COOLDOWN: 140 / 120 / 100
Active: Mordekaiser slows the target enemy champion, then banishes them with him to the Death Realm for a few seconds, cancelling all interactions with non-banished units.
Additionally, Mordekaiser heals himself for a portion of the target's maximum health and temporarily steals a portion of their current  attack damage,  ability power,  total attack speed,  maximum health,  armor,  magic resist, and size.
If the target dies while inside the Death Realm, Mordekaiser keeps his stolen stats until they respawn.
Death Realm: The Death Realm confines units in a small area. Allies and enemies cannot see or interact with those in the Death Realm but can see their locations and vice versa.
League of Legends content
 Tidecaller's Blessing COOLDOWN: 11
Active: Nami blesses herself or an allied champion for a few seconds, empowering their next three basic attacks or abilities to deal bonus magic damage and briefly slow enemies.
League of Legends content
 Piercing Darkness COOLDOWN: 15
Active: Senna fires a spectral laser toward the target unit. Senna and allied champions hit are healed. Enemies hit are dealt physical damage, which applies  life steal and on-hit effects, and slowed for a short time.
Piercing Darkness' cooldown is  reduced on-hit.
League of Legends content
 High Note COOLDOWN: 8 / 7.5 / 7 / 6.5 / 6
Active: Seraphine hurls a soundwave to the target location that quickly expands upon arrival, dealing magic damage to enemies within based on their missing health.
