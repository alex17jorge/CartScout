League of Legends V26.15

[edit | edit source]

Client

[edit | edit source]
Aegis of Valor
  • Aegis of Valor no longer reduces LP losses in the case of defeat, and wins award double LP without requiring a minimum mastery score.
League Classic
  • Our newest (oldest) game mode is coming to a server near you! Relive the glory days with League Classic. Marvel at what passed for modern graphical fidelity in the 2010s, outplay your opponents with point and click stuns and using old new items like Heart of Gold...!
  • Classic features 60 champions using classic versions of their gameplay across League's early history and is an experience that emphasizes strategy over mechanics and stronger consequences, both positive and negative, for your actions.
Ranked
  • Ranked Season 3 will start at 12 PM on July 29, 2026 local server time. Victorious Ranked Mission (15 Ranked Games won) will be refreshed with season start, and end of S2 rewards will be distributed shortly after S3 start. Shard transfer service will be temporarily off until S2 rewards are granted.
  • Apex Duo Restrictions
    • Master players will now be able to duo, but only with players close to them in rank.
    • GM, Challenger, and players with GM or Challenger MMR will not be allowed to duo.

Game

[edit | edit source]
Death Recap
  • Bug Fix: Fixed a bug where the Death Recap window may not appear when a player dies.
Role Quests
  • Bug Fix: Fixed a bug where Mid lane Tier 3 role quest boots could not be refunded with the undo button.

League of Legends
League of Legends (or LoL; initially League of Legends: Clash of Fates) is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) genre video game developed by Riot Games.

Platform
  • Microsoft Windows, macOS
Regular Map
  • Summoner's Rift

League of Legends content
Champion ability
A champion ability (often just ability) is a unique action performed by and only available to a specific champion. All of these special moves available to a champion collectively constitute their kit. A skill point is earned each time the champion  levels up, which the player can spend on most abilities to learn them and rank them up to improve their potency.

Champion abilities can have either or both active and passive effects:

  • Active effects are cast by using their respective hotkey and selecting a valid target. Most abilities require spending an amount of resource, such as  mana.
  • Passive effects may simply be permanently available or are cast by fulfilling a requirement or using a specific command.
Most abilities incur a  cooldown after being cast, during which they cannot be used. This cooldown time is usually reduced by  ability haste. Spell effects may trigger only when a valid ability deals damage. On-cast effects trigger upon an ability's cast time completing.

League of Legends content
Stack
A stack refers to a buff or debuff that can be increased in count under certain circumstances. Stacking effects with durations will refresh per instance, unless otherwise stated.

Stacking effects that time out may cause all stacks to expire at once or one by one in given intervals, depending on the source.

League of Legends content
Ghosted

A unit that is ghosted ignores unit collision, allowing them to move through other units and other units to move through them.

League of Legends content
Basic attack
The basic attack (or "auto") is the default means by which a unit deals damage, usually equaling  100% AD physical damage. Basic attacks are normally unit-targeted edge range spells, and the  attack range stat determines their reach.

The caster must stand still to complete a brief windup before launching a basic attack. Depending on the caster:

  • If a caster's attacks are non-projectile (e.g. melee) launching the attack deals damage immediately.
  • If a caster's attacks are projectile, launching the attack sends a projectile that follows the target and deals damage upon striking them.
The caster then incurs a brief cooldown, based on their  attack speed. Basic attacks' damage may trigger on-hit effects only upon dealing damage. On-attack effects will always trigger upon the attack's windup completing.

League of Legends content
Melee

Melee champions have a basic attack  range usually within 200 units. A melee attack does not use a projectile to deal damage, therefore not subject to effects that mitigate projectiles, like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for melee characters.

League of Legends content
Ranged

Ranged champions have a basic attack  range usually beyond 300 units. Many deal damage through projectiles, which are subject to mitigating effects like Wind Wall. Some champions can shift between a ranged and melee form, and certain items/runes differ for ranged characters.
